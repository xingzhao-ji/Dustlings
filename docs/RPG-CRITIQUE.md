# Independent critique — Dustlings RPG opening

## Review status, 2026-09-10

The new owner direction supersedes the cleanup-only courtyard scope. The target is an immersive, character-driven, real 3D cozy RPG for Steam: Stardew Valley informs attachment, residents, personal goals and discovery; Sky informs atmosphere, cohesion and emotional care. The first complete neighborhood must integrate on-foot play, a home, four substantial residents, a developing relationship, a restoration that changes daily life, a useful companion ability and an authored hidden discovery. This author is the independent AI critic, separate from the implementation team.

**The latest affected-interval review below confirms clearer local birth, smaller dialogue, a real moth and restrained light. It finds a visible companion discontinuity during the moth gift and identifies resident acting and arrival composition/material identity as the next major presentation hypotheses. The latest scripted route fails before the alcove and shared table, so their prior findings remain open. Human sessions remain 0; current RPG owner acceptance and presentation acceptance are absent.** The initial criteria below were written before RPG media arrived; they are risks, not observed player failures. I read the historical continuation, team agreement, earlier critique and courtyard protocol. I inspected the actual 1280×800 courtyard completion capture at `evidence/3d/courtyard-ui-review/completed.png`. That image establishes a visual baseline only. I did not operate the native game, listen to audio, or perceive continuous gameplay.

The old still shows coherent cream/sage materials, grounded shadows, small sprouted companions, recognizable houses and an unobstructed central landmark. These are foundations worth carrying forward. Its exposed diorama floor, detached houses and overhead workforce controls do not demonstrate inhabitable streets, interiors, comfortable third-person movement or resident attachment. Existing courtyard technical passes cannot be transferred to the new locomotion, camera, interaction or relationship systems.

A compact continuous route should show ordinary locomotion, an interaction choice, a resident's activity and conversation, companion help, and entry/exit at home. Label every prepared relationship state, teleport, accelerated clock, generated input or injected inventory condition. Prepared states are useful comparisons, but cannot prove the natural campaign sequence.

The consequential before/after comparison is **life before and after the accomplishment**, at the same location, rather than merely old art versus new art. Show the new opportunity being used and the saved state after returning. Include a close companion interaction and a crowded street view; the old 50-helper test does not cover new third-person occlusion, residents or interiors. Performance must name hardware, viewport, build and conditions and distinguish actual wall-clock callbacks from MovieMaker timing.

The independent review will report observed evidence separately from interpretations and specific follow-up checks. Development agents must not edit this verdict or treat its approval as the acceptance target. A technically complete route can still have shallow residents, obvious secrets or weak agency. Conversely, an AI preference for a different color or line of dialogue is not enough reason to replace work humans value.

Before gathering new data, define a bounded RPG observation segment and preserve the actual participants' build, device, prior exposure and play history. Existing agreed numerical rules remain historical/internal requirements where applicable, not a substitute for the newly requested outcomes.

Observe uncoached movement and interaction, which activity players choose, whom they revisit without being directed, whether consequences are noticed, and whether clues prompt their own experiments. After play, ask for specific confusing, tiring, unpleasant, disappointing and memorable moments. Use a neutral continuation opportunity, record exact counts and reasons, and do not infer attachment from compliance with the requested segment. Keep owner review of the current experience separate. No gate has passed.
