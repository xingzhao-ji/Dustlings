## Short description

Brush forgotten corners into tiny companions. Make a home in Bellwether, help four neighbours reconnect, and follow an old melody toward a hidden discovery in this gentle third-person adventure.

## About this neighborhood

### A little care changes a place

Dust settles. A small creature stirs. Your first companion follows you into Bellwether—a village with a broken crossing, an overgrown glasshouse, and neighbours with unfinished stories.

Walk through the neighborhood at your own pace. Brush away neglect, lend a hand to Mara's repairs, deliver Ivo's parcel, tend Nell's garden and make room at Tavi's table. Each act leaves something visible behind: a route opens, flowers return, a neighbour joins the others, or a keepsake finds its place at home.

Your companions do more than follow. A familiar tune and a little borrowed light can reveal what the village has kept quiet.

### In the playable neighborhood

- Explore Bellwether in third person, with an adjustable camera.
- Meet four named residents and complete their linked personal stories.
- Restore the crossing and garden, then bring the neighbours together for tea.
- Discover living dust companions and an ability that opens a hidden place.
- Choose home details and bring earned keepsakes back with you.
- Play offline, return to local saves, and keep extra save copies.
- Adjust music, effects, ambience, camera sensitivity and gentle motion.
