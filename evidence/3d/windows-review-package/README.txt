Dustlings - courtyard development review
Version 0.4.0-dev

Extract the entire ZIP to one folder, then open Dustlings.exe.
Keep Dustlings.exe and Dustlings.pck together. No installer is required.

Brush: hold the left mouse button and sweep over dusty paving.
B: Brush mode. D: Direct mode, then choose open paving for your crew.
U: Discoveries/upgrades. N: find remaining dust.
Arrow keys: aim. Hold Space: brush. Enter: direct at the aimed point.
Mouse wheel or +/-: zoom. Right/middle drag: move the camera.
Home or Frame: frame the courtyard. Esc: settings/pause or return.
Settings includes sound levels, gentle motion and save-copy controls.

This is a work-in-progress single-player courtyard review, not a finished
commercial release. The village's later zones are not included.
Archive verification does not establish Windows execution, performance,
player satisfaction, owner acceptance or store readiness.
