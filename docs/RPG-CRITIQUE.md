# Independent critique — Dustlings RPG opening

## Review status, 2026-09-10

The new owner direction supersedes the cleanup-only courtyard scope. The target is an immersive, character-driven, real 3D cozy RPG for Steam: Stardew Valley informs attachment, residents, personal goals and discovery; Sky informs atmosphere, cohesion and emotional care. The first complete neighborhood must integrate on-foot play, a home, four substantial residents, a developing relationship, a restoration that changes daily life, a useful companion ability and an authored hidden discovery. This author is the independent AI critic, separate from the implementation team.

**The first arrival recording has now been reviewed below. It establishes a closer real 3D scene, but exposes an unreadable opening panel and weak dust/prop affordances. It does not demonstrate the RPG loop. Human sessions remain 0; current RPG owner acceptance and presentation acceptance are absent.** The initial criteria below were written before RPG media arrived; they are risks, not observed player failures. I read the historical continuation, team agreement, earlier critique and courtyard protocol. I inspected the actual 1280×800 courtyard completion capture at `evidence/3d/courtyard-ui-review/completed.png`. That image establishes a visual baseline only. I did not operate the native game, listen to audio, or perceive continuous gameplay.

The old still shows coherent cream/sage materials, grounded shadows, small sprouted companions, recognizable houses and an unobstructed central landmark. These are foundations worth carrying forward. Its exposed diorama floor, detached houses and overhead workforce controls do not demonstrate inhabitable streets, interiors, comfortable third-person movement or resident attachment. Existing courtyard technical passes cannot be transferred to the new locomotion, camera, interaction or relationship systems.

## Five highest-impact risks

| Priority | Risk and player benefit of resolving it | Smallest useful implementation or diagnostic | Observable review criterion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Movement and framing make the village harder to inhabit.** A close following camera can trade the old stable spatial clarity for occlusion, wall clipping, abrupt correction or difficulty selecting nearby people. Comfort and reliable action are prerequisites for every relationship and discovery. | Integrate one ordinary route from home through a narrow street, past residents and companions, into an interior and back. Include a wall approach, turn, doorway and interaction with two nearby candidates. Use normal speed and the intended default camera; do not conceal corrections with cuts. | The player's body and intended target remain identifiable; walls do not swallow the camera; transitions preserve orientation; prompts follow a predictable target; dialogue does not also move the player or trigger another action. Ask physical players where control or orientation felt awkward. Automated routes and stills cannot establish comfort. |
| 2 | **Four residents become interchangeable quest dispensers.** A name, outfit and request are insufficient for attachment. Players need evidence that someone has a life, priorities and an evolving response to them. | Show one resident before helping, during a self-directed activity, after an accomplishment and on a later voluntary revisit. Show the other three in distinct activities and situations, with concise introductions that express more than task instructions. Build depth within the small cast before adding residents. | Review whether identity can be inferred from activity, place, body language and words together. Each resident should have a specific concern; one developing relationship must change an invitation, activity, knowledge or access, with an acknowledgement that fits that resident. Ask unfamiliar players whom they remember and why, without supplying names or traits first. Do not count affection meters or dialogue quantity as attachment. |
| 3 | **Accomplishments update flags while daily life stays the same.** If restoration only changes a mesh and a journal line, personal projects do not establish lasting connection. | Capture the same place and resident behavior before and after one restoration, then revisit after saving/reloading. Demonstrate one practical possibility that the change enables. Let the player choose between at least two understandable current activities without losing progress for choosing either. | The new route, shared activity, service, routine or personal access is visible in normal play and survives return. Players can explain what their action made possible and whose life changed. Record which goal they chose and their reason; an available button alone is not meaningful agency. |
| 4 | **The hidden discovery is either arbitrary or spoiled by task instructions.** A secret is unsatisfying when it requires unexplained repeated interaction, or when the journal exposes its entire solution before curiosity can work. | Author one clue-to-payoff chain using the same neighborhood: a situated clue, a relationship-derived piece of knowledge, a recognizable opportunity to apply it and a reveal with a small story or encounter. Show the relevant location before the player has the ability and after they return. | The clue can be encountered before success; the later opportunity remains recognizable without a secret marker; a mistaken attempt responds gently and does not consume scarce progress; the reveal changes what companions or players can subsequently do. Ask discoverers what made them try it and what surprised them. Record non-discoverers and assistance, rather than converting coaching into discovery success. |
| 5 | **The RPG layer loses Dustlings' distinctive companionship and becomes a menu of chores.** More tasks and text can dilute the tactile dust-to-life promise and quiet atmosphere that should connect the entire world. | Keep one close, uninterrupted sequence of brushing, local formation, first expressive acknowledgement, helpful action and later use of an unlocked companion ability. Place an optional home choice in the world and make its result persist. Use a compact contextual interaction scheme, with no full-screen checklist required for ordinary traversal. | At normal view size, the companion's origin, intention and contribution are readable; the new ability opens a useful possibility beyond a confirmation popup; home personalization is visible on return. Check whether dialogue and UI return promptly to the living scene, audio leaves quiet space and repeated companion cues remain comfortable. Human observation and listening are necessary to establish charm, fatigue and desire to return. |

## First integrated submission

Submit an exact playable build identity with normal-size, source-bound captures and one short interaction recording. A compact continuous route should show ordinary locomotion, an interaction choice, a resident's activity and conversation, companion help, and entry/exit at home. Submit the longer relationship/restoration/secret route separately if it would make that short recording unreadable. Label every prepared relationship state, teleport, accelerated clock, generated input or injected inventory condition. Prepared states are useful comparisons, but cannot prove the natural campaign sequence.

The consequential before/after comparison is **life before and after the accomplishment**, at the same location, rather than merely old art versus new art. Show the new opportunity being used and the saved state after returning. Include a close companion interaction and a crowded street view; the old 50-helper test does not cover new third-person occlusion, residents or interiors. Performance must name hardware, viewport, build and conditions and distinguish actual wall-clock callbacks from MovieMaker timing.

The independent review will report observed evidence separately from interpretations and specific follow-up checks. Development agents must not edit this verdict or treat its approval as the acceptance target. A technically complete route can still have shallow residents, obvious secrets or weak agency. Conversely, an AI preference for a different color or line of dialogue is not enough reason to replace work humans value.

## Human evidence still needed

The historical cleanup playtest's five-minute upgrade endpoint was designed for a finite cleaning slice. It does not measure this RPG opening's attachment or discovery. Before gathering new data, define a bounded RPG observation segment and preserve the actual participants' build, device, prior exposure and play history. Keep unfamiliar cozy/life-sim/RPG players identifiable; retain incremental buyers and friends/developers as separately reported or overlapping segments rather than silently pooling a changed audience. Do not retrofit the old 80% upgrade count into proof of RPG satisfaction. Existing agreed numerical rules remain historical/internal requirements where applicable, not a substitute for the newly requested outcomes.

Observe uncoached movement and interaction, which activity players choose, whom they revisit without being directed, whether consequences are noticed, and whether clues prompt their own experiments. After play, ask for specific confusing, tiring, unpleasant, disappointing and memorable moments. Use a neutral continuation opportunity, record exact counts and reasons, and do not infer attachment from compliance with the requested segment. Keep owner review of the current experience separate. No gate has passed.

**Next critic action:** inspect the first integrated native RPG recording and exact package supplied by the lead; prioritize concrete traversal, targeting and resident-presence failures before broader expansion or subjective decoration.
