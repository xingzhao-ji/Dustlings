# Independent critique: first 3D courtyard, 0.4 review

**Latest reviewed evidence, 2026-09-10: surface continuity, worker separation, purchase feedback and the 1.12 camera with a split header improve the visible courtyard. Dust identity, individual working expression and the fountain sequence's long visually similar tail remain material concerns.** The appended follow-up records exact captures and supersedes the corresponding initial findings only for those captures. This is an independent AI visual critique, not human play, listening, presentation approval or owner acceptance. Human sessions remain **0**.

**Recorded-baseline verdict: visible 3D depth is established; the creature-and-cleaning experience still needs material presentation work.** The houses, fountain, curb and cast shadows read as volumes in a stable diorama. The strongest unresolved issues are the appearance of removable dust, merging worker silhouettes and the small scale of the character action. These are independent AI observations and aesthetic hypotheses. They do not pass player, presentation or owner acceptance. Human sessions remain **0**.

This review is authored separately from the development team. It changes no game code or assets. The initial assessment below applies only to the preserved opening capture; any later submission must be distinguished from it.

**Choreography and UI to preserve, with limits.** The 50 helpers move from prepared rows into two readable rings around the fountain. Early gathering contains temporary groups, but the final arrangement has substantially better individual silhouette separation than the original five-helper baseline. The camera stays stable during the ceremony, the basin gains an identifiable new color, and small flowers appear around the boundary. These support a coherent spatial event. They do not establish a lively 50-helper work simulation or benchmark-quality emotional payoff.

The split header leaves the architecture more exposed; tools remain compact along the lower edge. The bell button temporarily overlaps the rear roof, and the lower hint lies over the front edge of the stage, but both are readable in the reviewed views. The centered ending dialog interrupts the view of the fountain and crew in this captured version. The lead reports a later change to preserve the world and offer an optional recap; that change is **not assessed here**.

The provided file named `current-chorus/completion.png` is an earlier view without the ending dialog. It is **not dialog evidence**. The movie's [49-second frame](../evidence/3d/critic/followup/ritual-49-dialog.png) does show the dialog, followed by generated dismissal and a return to the restored courtyard. The [53-second result log](../evidence/3d/critic/followup/current-chorus/results.json) reports completion; its filename alone would not establish what a screenshot depicts.

**Disposition.** Preserve the surface continuity, improved spacing, specific discovery feedback, stable elevated camera and compact split header. Continue the courtyard review with the final default camera and an uninterrupted, audible bell-to-return sequence; prioritize dust identity and the visual timing of the reveal. A later completion change, final package, real-time performance profile, save/recovery result or sound revision needs its own evidence.
