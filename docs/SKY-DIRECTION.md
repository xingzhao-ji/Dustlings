## Original Dustlings translation

The following choices are our hypotheses for this game, not prescriptions from Sky’s authors:

- **Atmosphere through hierarchy:** keep cream stone, sage growth and terracotta roofs. Separate warm lit surfaces from cooler, deeper contact shadows. Reserve the clearest turquoise and light reflection for the restored fountain. Large shapes should read before individual flecks, joints or leaves.
- **Space that invites attention:** frame the whole working courtyard generously, keep its required edges reachable and avoid panels competing with the first helper. Opening the journal is an intentional short visit; closing it returns attention and focus to the same patch.
- **Care shown through actions:** the brush reveals a place and creates a companion. A helper acknowledges direction, turns toward its task, works and settles. Existing pairing and chorus should communicate cooperation through motion and spacing. Preserve the original scalloped body, mint leaves, feet and face.
- **Restoration as a perceptible change:** connect the existing fountain chorus, first water, plant growth and original musical arrangement into a clear sequence. Local changes should carry the reveal before adding more global effects. Let the player watch afterward without an expanding HUD or obligatory extra click.
- **Coherent controls:** use the same stroke weight, rounded surfaces, modest depth and state language for ordinary controls and panels. Distinguish active, available and completed states with shape/text as well as color. Preserve readable type, mute, gentle motion and keyboard support.

Do not import Sky screenshots, characters, silhouettes, music, logos or other assets into Dustlings. The cited images remain reference links only. Figma is unavailable; actual playable UI and captured gameplay remain the inspectable design fallback.
