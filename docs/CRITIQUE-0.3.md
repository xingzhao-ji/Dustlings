# Independent critique: courtyard 0.3, historical 2D baseline

**Verdict: retain this as control/readability evidence; it does not meet the owner's new requirement for a fully 3D village.** The compact interface and basic dust → helpers → discoveries loop are coherent enough to preserve as a baseline. The larger crew loses individual clarity, and the scene does not yet give its small characters the visual prominence or material distinction that the premise needs. Those are this AI critic's assessments, not findings from human players.

This review is independently authored. No game code or assets were edited. Human sessions remain **0**; player experience, presentation and owner acceptance have not passed. The development team's desired outcome did not determine this verdict.

**Preserve these strengths.** The three main tools occupy a compact, consistent strip with legible active states. The discovery drawer explains cost and benefit and explicitly reassures the player that helpers are retained. Purchase confirmations are concrete. A flag, destination ring, mode instruction and “Let the crew roam” control make the basic direction action reviewable. The sprout, face and handheld brush form a recognizable creature at larger capture scale, while the paving revealed by brushing is visibly different from untouched areas. Settings use plain labels and provide a clear route back.
