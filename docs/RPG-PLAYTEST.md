# Bellwether opening — private RPG observation protocol

Status: prepared before data. RPG participants0; owner acceptancepending. This replaces the old upgrade-centric courtyard observation task for the new RPG. Do not combine results from the two game directions or count generated-input recordings as people.

Use12–20 unfamiliar intended players. Record each participant's experience with cozy/life-sim RPGs, incremental games and cleanup games separately; identify friends/developers rather than treating them as representative buyers. Obtain consent for observations/recording and use pseudonyms. No outreach is authorized by this document.

Test the exact current native package. Record build manifest/hash, device/OS, input device, resolution, sound setup, accessibility settings and session date. Use a fresh RPG save without spoiling the greenhouse or giving a route. The observer should not coach unless the player requests help; record precisely what help was given.

Neutral prompt: “Spend15minutes in this neighborhood as you would in a game you had just started. You can choose what to do.” After15minutes: “You can stop here or keep playing. Either is useful to us.” Record voluntary active continuation, duration and reason; do not leave an unfinished paid requirement or observer encouragement that biases continuation. This duration is an untested RPG protocol hypothesis and must be amended before data if the opening cannot support it. Retain the earlier80% uncoached-upgrade rule only for historical courtyard studies; this RPG has a different interaction/progression model.

Observe first: movement/camera comfort; recognition that dust becomes a companion; ability to approach and leave conversations; whether residents can be distinguished by activity/personality; chosen goal and alternatives; bridge navigation and social consequences; attention to earned clues; stopping/revisiting and how the player finds the next possibility. Record hesitations, repeated commands, missed targets, accidental interactions, fatigue, nausea, unreadable text, visual clutter and unwanted audio repetition. Do not reveal secrets to manufacture a discovery success.

Ask afterward, without naming intended emotions: “What were you trying to do?” “Who do you remember, and why?” “Did something you did change the place?” “What was awkward, confusing or tiring?” “What surprised you?” “What would you choose to do next?” “Would you come back to this build? Why or why not?” Ask about sound, controls and appearance separately. Distinguish spontaneous reports from responses to questions.

Report exactn/N for independent control, first companion, self-chosen goal, observed consequence, requested help and voluntary continuation. Keep failures and reasons. Do not average different audiences into one invented market verdict. No automated score establishes beauty, attachment, surprise or enjoyment. Record disagreements; choose the smallest focused alternative that addresses the most consequential observed problem. Owner must review and accept the current experience separately. A successful prototype route does not establish the full campaign or Steam readiness.
